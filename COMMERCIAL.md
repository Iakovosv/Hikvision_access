# Commercial use, and support

This document explains what you may do with **Hikvision Access Control** for free, what
needs a licence, and how to get one. It is a plain-language summary; the binding terms are
in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Free, no permission needed

- Installing and running the integration, unmodified, on your own Home Assistant, for
  personal and non-commercial use.
- Reading the source, and opening issues or discussions to report problems or ask questions.
- Using the integration at home even if you happen to be a professional, as long as the
  installation itself is not part of a paid service.

## Needs a licence

- Installing or configuring the integration **for a client**, as part of a paid job.
- Shipping it as part of a product, an appliance, a pre-configured hub or a managed service.
- Modifying it, or taking parts of it into another project.
- Anything else in the list under clause 4 of [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## How to get one

Open a discussion in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Iakovosv/Hikvision_access/discussions) with the
subject **"Commercial licence"**, or open an issue at
[the issue tracker](https://github.com/Iakovosv/Hikvision_access/issues). If you prefer
email, use the address on the repository owner's profile.

In the first message, please say:

1. Who you are (company and a contact name).
2. What you install it on, and roughly how many sites or devices.
3. Whether you need the integration as it is, or a change to it.
4. Whether you want support, and over what period.

## What a licence can include

The exact scope is agreed per case, but a typical arrangement covers:

- **Right to deploy** on a named number of sites or devices, for a named period.
- **Support** over that period: a direct channel, priority on bugs, and help during
  installation.
- **Small changes** needed for your deployment (for example, a different terminal model or
  an extra field), if they are agreed up front.

Pricing depends on the number of sites, whether support is included, and whether the work
needs a change to the code. Quotes are given per request.

## Support for private users

Private users are welcome to open issues and discussions, and they get the same code as
everyone else. There is no paid tier for private use, and nothing here is behind a paywall.

This is an independent project. It is not made by, endorsed by, or supported by Hikvision.
"Hikvision" is a trademark of its owner and is used only to say what the integration talks to.

## Reporting unlicensed use

If you find this integration redistributed, forked, bundled into another product, or installed
for a client without a licence, please report it through the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/Iakovosv/Hikvision_access/issues) or to the address on the
repository owner's profile. A link and a screenshot are enough.

Redistribution is prohibited by [LICENSE](LICENSE), and GitHub will act on a copyright
complaint for a fork that strips the licence or the copyright notices. Reports are welcome;
they are also the only way the author finds out, because the integration runs locally and
sends nothing back.
